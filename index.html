Academic Framework

Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)

Astra follows a modern Choice Based Credit System that breaks away from traditional rote learning. Customize your degree with Core, Elective, and Skill-based courses aligned with industry demands.

  • Interdisciplinary Minors: Break boundaries. A Mechanical Engineering student can minor in Artificial Intelligence; a Computer Science student can minor in Business or Design.

  • Project-Based Learning: 40% of credits are earned through hands-on labs, makerspace building, and real-world projects, not just theory.

  • 6-Month Industry Immersion: The final semester is dedicated entirely to a mandatory paid internship or startup incubation in our E-Cell.
